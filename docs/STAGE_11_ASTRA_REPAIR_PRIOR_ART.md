`anticipated tariff architecture -> sunk installed composition -> relative profitability of tariff architectures -> ex-post architecture choice`,

## Hagiu source limitation and gate consequence

The strongest unresolved evidence item is Hagiu (2006). The publisher page confirms the published model's sequential seller-before-buyer structure, fixed and variable platform fees, and endogenous commitment to buyer prices. The accessible 60-page predecessor provides detailed timing: when commitment is feasible, a platform may announce all three prices in stage 1 or announce seller-side terms and wait until stage 2 to set the user price; developer participation is determined in the intervening strategic environment. This is enough to narrow the manuscript and to reject any generic timing/commitment novelty claim.

However, the exact published RAND version of record could not be retrieved in full in the current environment. The Wiley full PDF is restricted, and the available ResearchGate record offers only a request-full-text path. Under the repair instruction, this remains an unresolved literature-source item rather than being silently treated as a clean novelty pass.

**Gate implication:** mathematical and manuscript repairs can be completed within Stage 11, but Stage 12 must remain paused until the Hagiu published-full-text comparison is closed (or an equivalently authoritative complete published text becomes accessible and is checked). If that full comparison reveals the same reciprocal architecture-state-profitability loop with the two-threshold/mixing result, route back to Stage 6 rather than repairing prose.

## AI positioning

The current primitives are not AI-specific. Generative AI is a motivating enterprise-service application because integration can be sunk and usage-linked pricing is salient, but the theorem also applies to other digital services with the same timing and tariff technology. No new AI-specific primitive has been introduced in this repair. Whether the final title should remain GenAI-forward is explicitly deferred to Stage 12 after the Stage-11 literature blocker is closed.
